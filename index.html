Protocol

From PIX to DeFi Yield
in Four Steps

Bridging Brazil's regulated financial infrastructure into permissionless DeFi lending markets.

Step 01
💳
Deposit via PIX
Send BRL via PIX to the EFIX funding account. Funds received instantly through HausBank's payment infrastructure.
Step 02
🏦
EFIX Subscribes Fund Units
The EFIX group subscribes units of BTG Pactual Tesouro Selic FI RF Referenciado DI (min. 95% in LFTs), held by Develop Participações, an EFIX group affiliate.
Step 03
🪙
Mint efixDI Tokens
efixDI minted on Base after settlement, at the current NAV — not 1:1 with fund units. The NAV accrues CDI daily.
Step 04
⚡
Leverage on Morpho
Deposit efixDI as collateral on Morpho Blue (Base), borrow USDC, loop for enhanced yield.
Advantages

Why DI-Fund-Backed Collateral?

D+0 fund redemption eliminates FX hedge costs. Not a guaranteed product: no capital protection.

🛡️
Regulated Collateral
Token backed by units of BTG Pactual Tesouro Selic FI RF Referenciado DI, held by Develop Participações, an EFIX group affiliate; NAV-accreting, not 1:1 with fund units. Not a guaranteed product.
⏱️
D+0 Same-Day Redemption
Same-day liquidity enables instant collateral liquidation. Eliminates the need for expensive FX derivatives that cost 2-3% annually.
🌐
Issued on Base
efixDI lives on Base, where it is minted and used in DeFi; the legacy Polygon token is paused. Composable with Morpho, Aave, and beyond.
📊
Sustainable Yield Spread
Earns the spread between CDI returns (~15%) and USDC borrow costs (~1-5%). Structural, non-speculative yield independent of crypto markets.

Yield Enhancement Model

Each leverage loop multiplies exposure to the CDI-USDC spread. Protocol targets 2-3 loops for optimal risk-adjusted returns.
